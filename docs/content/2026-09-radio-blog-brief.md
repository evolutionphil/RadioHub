# Radio listening editorial brief — 25 September 2026

## Scope

Ten useful listening guides, each fully localized in en, es, fr, de, pt, it, ru, ar, zh, tr, ja, ko, hi and he. This is 10 editorial topics / 140 language variants, not 140 interchangeable keyword landing pages. Existing station and genre pages remain the destinations for immediate listening.

## Research limits and decisions

Public SERPs and keyword datasets identify topic opportunities, not audited current global volumes. No access to a current worldwide Keyword Planner export was available. Do not present a guaranteed top-ten ranking, invented difficulty, measured audience size or promised traffic. The public [SEOpital radio keyword list](https://www.seopital.co/blog/the-best-radio-seo-keywords) (dated 9 January 2025) supplies directional seeds: online radio, internet radio, radio app, rock, jazz, blues, country and talk radio. Its numbers are not independently verified or used as current volume claims.

Radio.net's [listening directory](https://www.radio.net/), [mobile help](https://www.radio.net/faq_mobile_iphone_en_US), [data-usage guidance](https://radio.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/226776107-Data-usage), and radio.co's [internet radio guide](https://www.radio.co/blog/free-internet-radio) confirm practical listening intent, connection requirements and recurring setup questions. These are research inputs, not copy sources. No text is copied and no unsupported product features are claimed.

## Editorial and technical contract

- A complete, original useful article per topic and locale: introduction, at least four distinct sections, practical steps/checklists, two answered questions and three relevant localized internal links. Localize semantics, not just headers. Latin-language target roughly 350–550 words; other scripts comparable depth, not meaningless word-count padding.
- Never fabricate authors, listener tests, station availability, rankings, feature support, listening statistics or search volume. Visible organizational attribution: Mega Radio. Disclose AI-assisted preparation; do not claim a human editorial review that did not occur.
- Use original lightweight topic-specific illustrations, descriptive localized alt text, explicit dimensions and responsive image sizes. Illustrations contain only the invariant MegaRadio brand/issue number, never translatable editorial text. No stock licensing guesses or remotely hotlinked images.
- Per-language self-canonical, reciprocal 14-language hreflang plus English x-default, language/RTL direction, localized title/description, BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList, visible matching metadata and sitemap image entries. No fake reviews or unsupported rich-result promises.
- Sources: [Google Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article), [localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions), [image SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images), [helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). Technical eligibility is not an indexing, ranking or rich-result guarantee.
- Keep content out of the ordinary radio player bundle. No paid model/background translation jobs, schema migrations or overwriting station descriptions are required.
